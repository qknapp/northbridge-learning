About

Hi, I'm Quintin, founder of Northfield Learning. I hold an MSc in Chemistry and have experience working in both academic and industrial research laboratory environments. After working as a research chemist in industrial laboratories, I created Northfield Learning to bring real-world scientific experience into tutoring. My goal is to help students understand not only what to learn, but why concepts work.

I specialize in helping Ontario high school students succeed in Grade 11 Chemistry (SCH3U), Grade 12 Chemistry (SCH4U), and senior mathematics courses including Functions, Advanced Functions, and Calculus & Vectors. I also support first-year university chemistry courses, including general chemistry.

Based in Waterloo, Ontario, I provide online tutoring for students throughout Ontario, with in-person sessions available locally.

Why Choose Northfield Learning?

  • Advanced Expertise: I hold an MSc in Chemistry with a strong background in mathematics, scientific problem-solving, and chemical concepts.
  • Dual Experience: I have experience in both academic laboratories and industrial chemistry environments.
  • Tailored Approach: Sessions are custom-mapped to each student's unique learning style, curriculum pacing, and academic goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What subjects do you tutor?

Northfield Learning provides tutoring for Ontario students in Grades 9-12 mathematics, Grade 11 Chemistry (SCH3U), Grade 12 Chemistry (SCH4U), and first-year university chemistry.

Are sessions online or in-person?

Standard sessions are conducted online via an interactive digital classroom. However, if you are located within Waterloo and prefer in-person tutoring, please reach out!

How long are tutoring sessions?

Most tutoring sessions are 60 minutes, though longer sessions may be available for exam preparation or intensive review.

What happens during a tutoring session?

Sessions are tailored to each student’s goals and may include concept review, homework support, practice problems, test preparation, and study strategies.

Do you help students who are struggling?

Yes. Tutoring is designed to support students at a wide range of confidence and skill levels. The goal is to create a supportive environment where students can build understanding gradually and ask questions comfortably.

How does scheduling work?

Sessions may be booked weekly or as needed depending on availability. Advance scheduling is recommended during busy periods such as midterms and exam season.

How does payment work?

Payment is typically made by Interac e-Transfer. Additional payment details and policies are provided before tutoring begins.

What is your cancellation policy?

I request at least 24 hours' notice for any cancellations or session rescheduling. Cancellations or no-shows made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a session fee to respect scheduled blocks.
